Founded by Dr. Patrick McNutt, Coalesce Biosciences operates at the intersection of academic rigor and translational impact, bridging the gap between basic toxicological research and applied countermeasure development.

Based in Winston-Salem's Innovation Quarter, our infrastructure is purpose-built for agents that require the highest levels of containment, precision, and regulatory compliance.
